Entry URL to give the provider

{{ public_url }}?{{ panel.param }}=[respondent id]

Respondents arriving without a valid token are stopped before the questionnaire and sent to the screenout URL, and a token that has already produced a response is refused. To walk the questionnaire yourself, append ?preview=1 while signed in.

Sending a respondent to screenout or quota full

Set _outcome anywhere in the questionnaire and the return URL follows it. The usual case is a consent or eligibility gate:

"triggers": [{
  "type": "setvalue",
  "expression": "{consent} empty",
  "setToName": "_outcome",
  "setValue": "screenout"
}]

Accepted values are complete (the default), screenout and quotafull. The response is stored either way, so refusals stay in the data.
