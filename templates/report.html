Results report

An ordered document of blocks: text you write, and charts of the answers. Start empty and add what you want, or drop in all questions for the whole questionnaire in one block — that one keeps up by itself when the questionnaire grows. Each block says how far it may travel; the report's own setting is the ceiling for all of them.
